# Sources for the Viet Nam life tables

Every file in this folder is generated by `npm run life:data` (`scripts/life-data/build.mjs`) from the public sources below. Don't edit the JSON by hand; change the script and rebuild. The engine's lives are simulated, not real people, records or forecasts.

| File | Source | Licence | Transform |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mortality.json` | United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Population Division (2024). *World Population Prospects 2024*, complete life tables by single age, medium variant: `WPP2024_Life_Table_Complete_Medium_{Female,Male}_{1950-2023,2024-2100}.csv.gz` | CC BY 3.0 IGO | Viet Nam rows only. `qx` for ages 0–99 and `mx` for the open group 100+, rounded to 4 significant digits. The build stops if rounding moves life expectancy by 0.05 years or more in any year. |
| `fertility.json` | Same publication, fertility by single age, medium variant: `WPP2024_Fertility_by_Age1.csv.gz` | CC BY 3.0 IGO | Viet Nam, medium variant only. `ASFR` for ages 15–49 divided by 1,000 (births per woman per year), rounded to 4 significant digits. |
| `indicators.json` | Same publication, demographic indicators, medium variant: `WPP2024_Demographic_Indicators_Medium.csv.gz` | CC BY 3.0 IGO | Viet Nam rows: `SRB`, `TFR`, `LExFemale`, `LExMale`, `Births`, `Deaths`, rounded to 4 significant digits. Used for sex at birth, checks and the war-death share. |
| `crises.json` | Same publication, metadata for mortality crises: `4_Metadata/WPP2024_F02_Metadata.xlsx`, sheet `Mortality_Crises` (sources it cites include Rummel 1997, PRIO Battle Deaths 3.1, EM-DAT and WHO) | CC BY 3.0 IGO | Viet Nam rows grouped into `conflict` (event types Conflict and Battle deaths, added together), `disaster` (all Natural Disaster types), `epidemic` and `covid`. Each kind's share is its deaths divided by WPP's total deaths that year. The handful of conflict deaths listed from 2010 on are violence, not war. |
| `school.json` | UNESCO Institute for Statistics, completion rates `CR.1`–`CR.3` with income-fifth (`.Q1`–`.Q5`), town/village (`.URB`/`.RUR`) and sex (`.F`/`.M`) breakdowns, from `api.uis.unesco.org`; World Bank WDI `SE.PRM.CMPT.ZS`, `SE.SEC.CMPT.LO.ZS`, `SE.TER.ENRR` | CC BY-SA 4.0 (UIS) and CC BY 4.0 (WDI). **This file is released under CC BY-SA 4.0** because it adapts UIS data. | Viet Nam values in percent, as published, by survey or reference year; empty series dropped. |
| `work.json` | World Bank WDI: employment by sector `SL.{AGR,IND,SRV}.EMPL[.FE/.MA].ZS` (ILO modelled estimates), `NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.KD`, income shares `SI.DST.*.20`, `SI.POV.GINI` | CC BY 4.0 | Values as published, by year. |
| `marriage.json` | United Nations, DESA, Population Division (2019). *World Marriage Data 2019*, `undesa_pd_2019_wmd_marital_status.xlsx`, sheet `EVER_MARRIED` | CC BY 3.0 IGO | Viet Nam, 5-year age groups 15–59 plus 60+, as shares. One source per sex and year, preferring censuses, then the annual population change survey, then DHS, MICS and AIS. |
| `migration.json` | United Nations, DESA, Population Division (2024). *International Migrant Stock 2024*, `undesa_pd_2024_ims_stock_by_sex_destination_and_origin.xlsx`, `Table 1`; World Bank WDI `SP.URB.TOTL.IN.ZS` | CC BY 3.0 IGO; CC BY 4.0 | People born in Viet Nam by country of residence, 1990–2024 (destinations under 10,000 in 2024 grouped as Other); urban share by year. |

Download base for all WPP files: `https://population.un.org/wpp/assets/Excel%20Files/1_Indicator%20(Standard)/CSV_FILES/`.

**War deaths.** WPP adds excess deaths from conflicts, disasters and epidemics to its age- and sex-specific mortality for 1950–2023 (WPP 2024 Methodology Report, §I.C.3). So the 1950–1975 war deaths are already inside `mortality.json`; the engine must never add a separate war-death hazard on top.

World Bank and UIS responses are cached in `.cache/life-data/api/`, so a rebuild repeats the same numbers. Delete that folder to pick up new releases.

**Gaps the engine fills, never this folder.** Sector shares start in 1991 and GDP in 1990; UIS completion starts in 2000 (WDI has one 1979 point); marriage starts in 1988; migrant stock starts in 1990. Engine code holds the first observed value for earlier years and treats it as an estimate. The Maddison Project and Wittgenstein Centre could fill those years if their licences allow; they are not used yet.
